FAQ

Questions, answered.

What is TallyBuck?

TallyBuck is a rewards platform where registered users can discover and complete eligible activities for rewards.

How do I start earning?

Create an account, sign in and open the available task section to see activities currently available to you.

Do all users see the same tasks?

No. Available opportunities may vary according to factors such as location, device and eligibility.

When are rewards credited?

Rewards are credited after the applicable activity has satisfied its verification requirements.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10, subject to the applicable withdrawal requirements and review.

Does TallyBuck require deposits?

No. TallyBuck does not require users to deposit money in order to use the rewards platform.
