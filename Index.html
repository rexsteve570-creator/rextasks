Questions

Before you sign up.

Is RexTasks free to join?

Yes. Creating an account and using the platform is free. RexTasks does not currently require users to make a deposit to participate.

How do I earn money on RexTasks?

You can earn by completing available surveys, tasks, offers and daily activities. The tasks available to you depend on your country and current task inventory.

How much can I earn?

Earnings depend on the tasks available to your account, the reward assigned to each task and how many eligible tasks you successfully complete. Earnings are not guaranteed.

What is the minimum withdrawal?

The minimum withdrawal amount is $10.

Can I withdraw to my bank account?

Bank transfer withdrawals are available to users in Nigeria. Users outside Nigeria can currently use cryptocurrency withdrawal options.

Do I get paid immediately after completing a task?

Not necessarily. Some tasks require verification. For manual tasks, submitted proof may be reviewed by the RexTasks team before the reward is added to your wallet.

Is RexTasks available worldwide?

RexTasks is designed for users worldwide. However, available tasks and offers can vary significantly depending on your country.

How does the referral reward work?

You can invite people using your referral link. The $0.10 referral reward becomes eligible when the referred user completes at least one eligible and verified task.

Do I need to deposit money?

No. RexTasks currently operates without user deposits.
